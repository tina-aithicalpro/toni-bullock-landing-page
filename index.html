About

Toni Bullock is an Emmy® Award-winning producer and creative entrepreneur with a passion for storytelling and curating intentional, unforgettable experiences. With over a decade of experience in media and entertainment, she has built a career developing impactful content and launching brands that connect, inspire, and elevate. Toni has produced and developed content for major platforms including Netflix, Bravo, CBS, OWN, Meta, and BuzzFeed. Her credits include Black Women Own the Conversation (OWN), Red Table Talk (Facebook), The Amazing Race (CBS), Peace of Mind with Taraji, Rhythm and Flow, and Sherri. Her expertise spans unscripted development, casting, and field production, and she’s known for shaping stories that resonate with diverse, forward-thinking audiences.

In 2017, Toni founded Callsheets 2 Cocktails (C2C), a professional community created to connect, educate, and empower women in media. Her motto: “Networking isn’t just something that happens in bars, it can happen anywhere.” Through curated panels, an international retreat in Bali, workout sessions, networking events across New York, LA, and Atlanta, and educational brunches, C2C has grown into a trusted space for women across the industry to build relationships and thrive.

After years of coordinating standout events and global retreats through C2C, Toni launched Effortlessly Coordinated, a boutique event coordination company serving the NYC Tri-State area and available for travel, specializing in weddings, private events, and corporate experiences. Whether she’s producing a television special or planning a milestone celebration, Toni brings the same attention to detail, creativity, and seamless execution to every project. Toni is also a sought-after speaker, sharing insights on entrepreneurship, creative leadership, women’s empowerment, and navigating the media industry. Her ability to blend authenticity with strategy has made her a trusted voice for both emerging creatives and established professionals. Now based in New York, Toni spends her spare time hiking, traveling, and enjoying quality time with family and friends.
